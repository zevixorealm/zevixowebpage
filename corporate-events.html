Where is Zevixo Realm located in Hyderabad?

Zevixo Realm is inside Prasads IMAX Mall, NTR Gardens, Khairatabad, Hyderabad, Telangana 500063. Open Google Maps directions.

What can our corporate group do at Zevixo Realm?

Your team can explore the Immersive LED Room, where guests wear illuminated jackets and goggles, and take on the LaserMaze challenge. Share your group's interests so the team can discuss suitable options.

Is Zevixo suitable for a short team outing or after-work activity?

Zevixo is an indoor entertainment destination in central Hyderabad, making it an option to consider for a shorter group visit as well as a planned team outing. Share your preferred time and date to discuss availability.

Can we bring a large team?

Group arrangements depend on the number of participants, date and session availability. Send your approximate headcount first so the team can advise on the options.

Do you offer corporate packages or group pricing?

Corporate group options and pricing should be confirmed directly with the Zevixo team. Submit your requirements to request current details. No package price is assumed on this page.

How far in advance should we enquire?

For a preferred date or a larger group, enquire as early as possible. If your date is flexible, include a preferred week or month in your message.
